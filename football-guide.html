Football Rules and Scoring: A Beginner's Guide

Football is a team sport played between two teams. Each team normally has 11 players on the field, including a goalkeeper. The main objective is to score more goals than the opposing team.

1. How Long Is a Football Match?

A standard senior football match lasts 90 minutes, divided into two 45-minute halves. There is normally a halftime break. The referee may add stoppage time for delays such as substitutions, injuries, and other interruptions.

Some competitions use extra time or a penalty shootout when a winner is required and the score is tied. The exact rules depend on the competition.

2. How Are Goals Scored?

A goal is scored when the whole ball crosses the goal line between the goalposts and beneath the crossbar, provided no rule has been violated. The team with more goals at the end wins.

  • 1–0: One team has scored one goal and the other has scored none.
  • 2–1: The first team has scored two goals and the second team has scored one.
  • 0–0: Neither team has scored a goal.

3. Common Football Rules

  • Kick-off: Starts each half and restarts play after a goal.
  • Throw-in: Restarts play when the ball completely crosses a touchline.
  • Corner kick: May be awarded when the defending team last touches the ball before it crosses its own goal line without a goal being scored.
  • Free kick: Awarded after certain offences.
  • Penalty kick: May be awarded for a direct-free-kick offence committed by a defending player inside their own penalty area, subject to the Laws of the Game.
  • Offside: An offence can occur when an attacker in an offside position becomes involved in active play. Being in an offside position alone is not an offence.

4. Understanding a Match Score

A live score usually shows the home team's goals and the away team's goals. The match status may say first half, halftime, second half, full time, or postponed. Always check the competition and match time because schedules can change.

5. How to Follow Football Responsibly

Check the competition's official website or a trusted sports provider for confirmed fixtures, line-ups, results, and disciplinary updates. Live scores can be delayed, so a displayed score may not always be instantaneous.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Can a football match end in a draw?

Yes. Many league matches can end in a draw. Knockout competitions may use extra time or penalties, depending on their rules.

What does full time mean?

Full time means the referee has ended the match, including the applicable added time.
