🏏 CRICKET GUIDE

What Is Run Rate in T20 Cricket?

By LGT Sports Desk · Educational guide

Run rate shows how many runs a team scores on average per over. It helps fans understand the scoring speed of a team during a cricket innings.

How is run rate calculated?

Divide the total runs scored by the number of overs faced. For example, if a team scores 48 runs in 6 overs, its run rate is 8 runs per over.

Example: 48 ÷ 6 = 8.00 runs per over.

Why does run rate matter?

Run rate gives a quick picture of scoring pace, but it does not tell the whole story. Wickets remaining, overs left, pitch conditions, and the target can all affect how a team should approach its innings.

In a chase, fans should also look at the required run rate: the runs still needed divided by the overs left. This shows the scoring pace needed to reach the target.

⚽ FOOTBALL GUIDE

What Is Pressing in Football?

By LGT Sports Desk · Educational guide

Pressing is a team tactic used to put pressure on opponents who have the ball. The aim may be to win possession back, force a rushed pass, or make the opponent play into a less dangerous area.

How does pressing work?

A player near the ball may close down the opponent, while teammates block passing options and move together to reduce available space. Effective pressing depends on timing, communication, and team shape.

High press and low block

A high press puts pressure on opponents closer to their own goal. A low block defends deeper and focuses on protecting space near the defending team's goal. Teams may change between these approaches depending on the opponent and match situation.

Pressing can create chances, but it also uses energy and may leave space behind the players who move forward. That is why coordinated movement matters.

📊 MATCH ANALYSIS GUIDE

How to Read a Match Preview

By LGT Sports Desk · Educational guide

A match preview helps fans understand the context around an upcoming game. A useful preview explains what to watch rather than simply predicting a winner.

1. Check recent form

Look at recent results and the quality of the opponents. A short winning or losing streak alone does not prove how a team will perform next.

2. Consider player availability

Injuries, suspensions, rotation, and confirmed team lineups can affect a match. Use reliable sources and distinguish confirmed information from rumours.

3. Understand the matchup

Consider playing style, defensive strengths, attacking options, and the importance of the fixture. In cricket, conditions, batting order, bowling options, and the format can also matter.

4. Verify dates and information

Match schedules and team news can change. Check the competition's official website or trusted sources for the latest confirmed details before sharing them.

A preview is an informed way to discuss a game, not a guarantee of its result.
