About the Nepal Premier League

The Nepal Premier League (NPL) is a professional franchise-based cricket competition in Nepal. It brings together teams, players and cricket supporters from different parts of the country. The tournament provides a platform for domestic cricket talent and helps grow interest in the sport.

Understanding T20 Cricket

T20 cricket is a limited-overs format in which each team normally gets a maximum of 20 overs to bat. The team scoring more runs wins the match, subject to the playing conditions. Overs, wickets, runs and the required run rate help visitors understand the progress of a match.

How to Read an NPL Live Score

The score usually shows the runs scored and wickets lost. For example, 145/4 means a team has scored 145 runs and lost four wickets. The overs figure shows how much of its innings has been completed. Score information may change as a match progresses.

What Does a Cricket Scorecard Show?

A batting scorecard lists runs, balls faced, boundaries and how each batter was dismissed. A bowling scorecard commonly shows overs, runs conceded, wickets and economy rate. The match summary provides the result and other key details.

How to Check Fixtures and Results

Fixtures show upcoming matches, including their scheduled dates and times. Results show the outcome of completed matches. Use the official NPL fixtures page linked above to check the latest confirmed schedule, scorecards and results.
